How to make Aunty Selmas fluffy pancakes

Ingredients

  1. A cup of flour
  2. 3 table spoons of baking powder
  3. 2 eggs
  4. 1/2 cup of sugar
  5. A table spoon of cinamon
  6. A table spoon of oil

Steps

  1. Crack to eggs in a bowl and whisk well
  2. Add 1/2 cup of sugar and continue the whisking
  3. Add your 1 cup of flour and mix thoroughly with a spatulla
  4. Add a table spoon of oil to the mixture and continue to mix it up
  5. Add 3 table spoons of baking powder and your cinamon.
  6. Heat up your pan and use oil or butter to fry your pancakes(I go for a nice light brown colour).
