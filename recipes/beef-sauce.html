How to make beef sauce

Ingredients

  1. A pack of ground beef
  2. Two fresh tomatoes
  3. An onion
  4. A sachet of tomato paste
  5. Pepper,MSG seasoning,curry and anyother choice spices

Steps

  1. Marinate your ground beef with the spices and let it sit.
  2. Heat up your pan and sizzle some onions on it.
  3. Mix your tomato paste with a little water and add it to the pan to fry with the onions.
  4. Add some spices to the pan
  5. Take the tomato paste and onions mix out of the pan and set aside.
  6. Stir fry your ground beef.
  7. When your grounf beef is cooked,you can combine with your tomato and onions mix
  8. Let it cook in the pan for a little bit,and you have beef sauce.
