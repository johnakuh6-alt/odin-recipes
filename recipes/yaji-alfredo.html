How to make hot Alfredo,the naija way.

Ingredients

  1. Offcourse yaji(A Nigerian sort off paprika spice),the base of this recipe
  2. Heavy cream,i live in Russia so i substitute heavy cream for a Russian milk product called slivki
  3. An onion
  4. Chicken(optional and very stressful)
  5. MSG seasoning,pepper and any other choice spices.B-ut not curry.
  6. Mozerela cheese

Steps

  1. Boil your pasta of choice(my favourite is penn)
  2. Slice your mozerella into cubes.
  3. Pour your heavy cream or slivki into a hot pan.
  4. Add your spices,most importantly your yaji
  5. Incorporate your sliced mozerella to the mix
  6. Add your pasta to the mix
