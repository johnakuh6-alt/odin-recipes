How to make hot Alfredo,the naija way.

Ingredients

  1. Offcourse yaji(A Nigerian sort off paprika spice),the base of this recipe
  2. Heavy cream,i live in Russia so i substitute heavy cream for a Russian milk product called slivki
  3. An onion
  4. Chicken(optional and very stressful)
  5. MSG seasoning,pepper and any other choice spices.--but not curry.
